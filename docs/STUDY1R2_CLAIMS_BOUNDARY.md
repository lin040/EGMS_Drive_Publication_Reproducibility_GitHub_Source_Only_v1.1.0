# Study 1-R2 claims boundary

Study 1-R2 is a post-hoc exploratory controlled-synthetic model-development
experiment. It was initiated only after the frozen Study 1-R result was known.
It does not replace, revise, suppress, or reinterpret the original Study 1-R
comparison, and it is not preregistered confirmation of the original
hypothesis.

The permissible claim, if the final R2 comparison is favorable, is limited to:

> A validation-selected Structured-R2 candidate improved one or more outcomes
> relative to Baseline B in a separate, once-only controlled-synthetic R2
> evaluation using new training, validation, offline-test, and rollout seeds.

The following claims are not supported:

- that the original Study 1-R hypothesis was confirmed;
- that the original adverse result was corrected or superseded;
- that Structured-R2 improves real-world driving safety;
- that results transfer to CARLA, public datasets, real vehicles, or deployment;
- that a favorable final result was guaranteed by the validator.

All final endpoints must be retained regardless of direction. The validator
checks completeness, hashes, seed separation, checkpoint replay, and raw-output
recomputation only; Structured-R2 may improve, tie, or underperform.
